## English

**App name:** SafeGuard: Content Protection

**Short description (73 characters):**
On-device content filtering: DNS blocking, SafeSearch and app protection.

**Full description:**

SafeGuard helps you keep unwanted content off an Android device. It works
on the device itself, with no account and no servers of ours.

WHAT IT DOES
• DNS protection: a local VPN checks domain names before your device
  connects, and blocks sites in the categories you choose (bundled lists
  for adult content, gambling and drugs).
• Search protection: enforces SafeSearch on Google and Bing and YouTube
  Restricted Mode through DNS, and checks searches made in SafeGuard's own
  search screen.
• App protection (optional): stops apps you choose from opening, using
  Android's Accessibility service to learn only which app opened.
• On-device AI: a small model classifies search text on your device.
  Nothing is uploaded.
• Your own lists: block or allow specific domains, and block keywords.
• PIN protection: turning protection off or loosening it needs your PIN,
  with a lockout after repeated wrong attempts.
• Modes: Normal, Strict or Custom.
• Statistics and a local log (kept 7 or 30 days, or not at all).

PRIVACY
• Everything stays on your device. No analytics, no ads, no trackers.
• SafeGuard sees domain names, not page content, messages or passwords.
• You can clear the log or delete all data at any time.

HONEST LIMITS
• No app can filter everything on Android. Apps or browsers that use
  their own encrypted DNS, another VPN, or changes to Android settings can
  get around SafeGuard.
• Posts inside apps like Instagram or TikTok can't be filtered; you can
  block the whole app instead.
• Block lists are compiled automatically and can be wrong; you can allow
  a site yourself.
• Only one VPN app can run at a time on Android.

PERMISSIONS
• VPN (required): local DNS filtering. Traffic isn't sent to any
  SafeGuard server.
• Accessibility (optional, only for App protection): learns the name of
  the app that opened. Doesn't read the screen or what you type.

**Feature list (for graphics / what's new):**
DNS filtering · SafeSearch · App protection · On-device AI · Custom
block/allow lists · Blocked keywords · PIN lock · Normal/Strict/Custom
modes · Statistics · Arabic and English

## Privacy explanation (for the privacy-policy page)

SafeGuard processes data only on your device.
- **Stored locally:** your settings, block/allow lists, keywords and
  protected apps; a protection log (time, category, source, and the
  blocked domain or protected app name; searches as a short
  non-reversible number), kept for the period you choose; a salted
  PBKDF2 hash of your PIN, encrypted with an Android Keystore key.
- **Never collected:** passwords, messages, photos, contacts, full
  browsing history, search text, screen or audio recordings.
- **Sent off the device:** nothing by SafeGuard. Allowed DNS lookups
  continue to your network's DNS servers, as they would without the app.
- **Deletion:** Settings → Privacy (clear log, delete all data), or
  Android's "Clear data". Uninstalling removes everything.
- **Backups:** app data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device
  transfer.

## Permission explanation (short, for the listing or FAQ)

- **VPN:** to check domain names locally and block the categories you
  chose. Required for DNS filtering and SafeSearch.
- **Accessibility (optional):** only for App protection, to know which
  app opened. Doesn't read content.
- **Start at boot:** to turn protection back on after a restart, if it
  was on.

## FAQ

**Does SafeGuard see my browsing?** It sees the domain names your device
looks up (like example.com), not pages, messages or passwords. It keeps
only blocked domains in its local log.

**Will it slow my phone?** Checking a domain takes microseconds in tests
on a computer. Real-phone measurements are pending (see
`docs/PERFORMANCE.md`), so we don't publish a number yet.

**Why doesn't it block everything on Instagram?** Posts come from
Instagram's own servers; no DNS filter can separate them. Use Instagram's
sensitive-content setting or protect the whole app.

**Can someone turn it off?** Turning it off in the app needs the PIN. The
device owner can still disconnect the VPN or uninstall the app in
Android settings; SafeGuard reports interruptions but can't prevent them.

**Can I use it with another VPN?** No. Android runs one VPN at a time.

**What if a site I need is blocked?** Add it to Allowed domains (PIN
required).
